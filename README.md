# Kallmann syndrome posts over time

Interactive mockup of 696 posts from r/kallmann_syndrome (Feb 2016 – Sep 2026),
made for the Health in Social Media data science capstone.

**Live page:** https://ethansoroko.github.io/health-social-media-viz/

- **Timeline:** posts per month. Drag across the overview strip, use the presets, or pick months to set the date range.
- **Hover** a month to see who posted (patient, family member, researcher, …) and what kind of post it was.
- **Click** a month to read sample posts from it, linked back to Reddit.

`index.html` is self-contained (D3 v7 from CDN). It is generated by
`Visualization/build_timeline.py` in the project's private analysis repo.
